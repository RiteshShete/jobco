JobCO

Your personal job-search board. Track every application from wishlist to offer, get an AI-written application kit for each job, and reach out to people who can refer you. It runs on your own computer, so your data stays with you.

  • Drag-and-drop board with follow-up reminders
  • Application kit: cover letter, resume bullets, interview questions, company brief
  • People panel with LinkedIn connections and email drafts

JobCO is a local app, so it can't run on this page. Ask the owner for the folder and double-click start.bat.
